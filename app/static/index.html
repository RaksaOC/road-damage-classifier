Road Damage Classifier
ML project · Fundamentals of Machine Learning

Find road damage, square by square

Upload a road photo. The model checks every square, names the damage it sees, and gives the road a repair priority.

The pipeline

  1. PhotoA phone or dashcam photo of the road.
  2. GridCut into square cells, each checked on its own.
  3. PreprocessGrayscale, resized to 32 × 32 = 1,024 numbers.
  4. FeaturesPCA plus 5 measurements: brightness, contrast, edge strength, dark area, edge direction.
  5. ClassifySoftmax regression, SVM and a neural network pick one of 5 classes.
  6. PriorityThe damage adds up to a 0–10 repair priority.

Photo upload arrives once the model is trained.

Data: RDD2022 road damage dataset Models: softmax regression, SVM, neural network
